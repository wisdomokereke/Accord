How conversations become legally enforceable agreements

01

Start a conversation

Open a thread with your client, partner, or contractor. Talk freely about deliverables, terms, and expectations without intimidating templates.

02

Accord drafts the terms

As terms emerge (like ₦350k for brand redesign across 4 weeks), Accord structures them into clean, precise clauses ready for review.

03

Review together in sync

Both parties preview the living Accord side-by-side. Suggest a change in chat, and watch the document update live with mutual consent.

04

Sign & keep a record

Execute with compliant cryptographic e-signatures. Receive audit receipts and store all historical records in your secure archive.
